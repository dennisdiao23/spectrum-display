Flip-Chip COB Technology

Superior surface protection, higher contrast, and better reliability compared with traditional SMD. Excellent performance under close viewing.

Front Service Design

Modules and power/data can be accessed from the front, simplifying installation and maintenance in wall-mounted applications.

High Refresh & HDR Ready

3840 Hz+ refresh rates for camera-friendly, flicker-free imagery. Supports high dynamic range content for impactful visuals.

Seamless 16:9 Cabinets

Optimized cabinet sizes for easy construction of standard 16:9 and ultra-wide video walls without awkward cropping.
